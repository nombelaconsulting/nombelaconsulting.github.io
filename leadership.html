Founder Profile

A strategist working at the intersection of finance, governance, philanthropy, and community-driven systems change.

With professional experience spanning London, New York, and Johannesburg, Ndumiso brings institutional-level financial and governance expertise into mission-driven work across Africa.

His work increasingly focuses on helping organisations build credible structures, strategic partnerships, and scalable systems capable of delivering meaningful social impact.

"The most durable change happens when communities are part of the architecture — not just the beneficiaries."

As Director of the One K Foundation, he designs impact metrics and financial sustainability strategies for public health initiatives, bridging the gap between capital and social outcomes. His work is further informed by active roles across Ubuntu Home Foundation, Mbali the Village, and Final Cry Ministries — giving him lived experience of the systems, governance, and funding challenges that mission-driven organisations face on the ground.

Social Impact Work

Beyond advisory practice, Ndumiso serves as a Director of the One K Foundation — bridging capital and social outcomes in public health and community development.

Global Commitment

The Jena Declaration

Nombela Consulting Group is a proud Institutional Signatory of The Jena Declaration — a global initiative convened under the UNESCO Chair on Global Understanding for Sustainability, committed to building a healthier, safer, and more sustainable world.

As a signatory, the firm stands alongside global institutions including the Club of Rome, UNESCO Chairs, and Henley Business School Africa in affirming that sustainable development requires the integration of governance, community, and long-term institutional commitment.
